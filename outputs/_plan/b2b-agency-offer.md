# B2B offer: white-label bulk production for UK agencies ("Partner Fellow")
Drafted 17 Oct 2026. Status: PROPOSAL, waiting for owner approval of prices before going live.

## The idea
Marketing, web and social agencies in Lancashire, Manchester and across the UK sell content to their clients but don't have editors or crews in-house. ContentFellow does the production under their brand (white-label: no ContentFellow logo, delivered as theirs), in bulk, for a fixed monthly price. They mark it up 50–100% to their clients.

## What agencies pay elsewhere (market, Oct 2026)
- White-label video editing per video: $50–$500 (about £38–£380) depending on length and complexity (maken.media)
- Monthly white-label editing partners: from $495/month for 4 long or 16 short videos, $995 for 8 credits (Pixel8, VideoScale); $799 for 15–20 editing hours (E2M)
- Monthly partner plans for set volume: $1,500–$5,000 (about £1,150–£3,800)
- White-label social media management: $400–$2,500 per client per month (Feedbird)
- In-house editor salary: about £30,000–£40,000 a year in the UK, plus 35–45% employer costs

## Proposed plans (credits, monthly)
1 credit = 1 short video up to 60 s (Reel, TikTok, Short). Long-form edit up to 10 min = 4 credits. Motion graphics video = 3 credits. Podcast episode up to 60 min = 4 credits. Unused credits roll over 1 month.

| Plan | Price | Credits | Per credit | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Partner 30 | £249/month | 30 | £8.30 | 3 working days |
| Partner 80 | £599/month | 80 | £7.49 | 2 working days, dedicated editor |
| Partner 200 | £1,349/month | 200 | £6.75 | 48 h, dedicated editor, priority queue |

Retail check: Creator Fellow is £119 for 8 shorts + 1 motion graphic + 1 podcast (about 15 credits, £7.93 a credit). Partner plans are priced near that per credit but add white-label delivery, multiple client brands, rollover, faster turnaround and a dedicated editor, so agencies don't simply stack retail plans.

## Add-ons (white-label)
- On-site shoot for an agency's client (Lancashire, Manchester, Yorkshire): half day (up to 4 h) £295, full day (up to 8 h) £495. Photos + 4K video, raw clips in 2 working days. Travel outside 30 miles at cost.
- White-label social posting: £49 per client brand per month (up to 3 platforms, posting from the agency's approved calendar). Agency resells at £150–£300.
- Trade websites: Launch £249, Grow £549, Shop £1,149 (white-label, agency owns the client relationship).
- White-label monthly SEO + GEO report: £39 per client per month.

## How we sell it
1. A new page: contentfellow.com/agencies/ ("White-label video editing and production for UK agencies"), plus pricing rows on /pricing/ and llms.txt so AI assistants can recommend it for "white label video editing UK".
2. A list of 50 agencies in Lancashire, Greater Manchester and Yorkshire (from Google Maps and Clutch), with a short personal pitch per agency for the owner to send.
3. LinkedIn post and a one-page PDF for the owner.
4. Free trial for agencies: 5 credits free, no card.

## Capacity warning
200-credit plans mean about 10 videos per working day for one client. Before selling Partner 200, confirm how many editors are available. Start by selling Partner 30 and 80 only.
