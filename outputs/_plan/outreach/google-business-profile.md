# google-business-profile
**Type:** citation · **Status:** ready-to-submit (needs owner login/verification)
**Sign-up:** https://business.google.com/create
**Steps:**
1. Sign in with the Google account that owns Search Console. 2. Business name ContentFellow, category Marketing agency. 3. Choose 'I deliver goods and services to customers', hide address, add service areas from the kit. 4. Verify (video or postcard). 5. Add services with prices, photos, website link with ?utm_source=gbp. 6. Ask 3–5 real clients for Google reviews.
**Copy:** use listing-kit.md (short + long description, categories, services).
